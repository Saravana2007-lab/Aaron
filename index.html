Doctor's Dietary Guidelines

Meal Frequency

Small frequent meals every 2–3 hours.

Hydration

Consume adequate fluids according to medical instructions.

Vegetables

At least half the plate should consist of non-starchy vegetables (bottle gourd, ridge gourd, okra, spinach, green beans).

Fruits

Eat a variety of fruits.

Whole Grains

Whole grains, millets, brown rice and quinoa.

Lean Protein

Skinless chicken, turkey, fish, eggs, legumes, pulses, tofu and nuts.

Healthy Fats

Nuts, avocado and reduced-fat dairy.

What to Avoid

Refined grains, added sugars, unhealthy fats, fried foods, high-salt foods and alcohol.

Doctor's instructions always take priority.
